# Plumbers on Call CORP — visual direction

This is the rule book for how the site looks. Every scene on every page is
checked against it. It was agreed with the owner on 2026-11-13 after reviewing
a screen recording of the whole site.

## Concept (locked)

**True to the line.** One brass line runs through a dark stone world. Each page
is a place on that line, and navigating means travelling along it.

Locked and not to be redesigned: the camera travel between pages, the camera
descent on scroll, the top of Home (the Name), the P-o-C intro, the header logo
plate. Timing may be tuned; the choreography stays.

## The seven rules

1. **One world, four materials.** Black marble with gold veining, ivory stone
   (the logo's stone), brushed brass, smoked glass. Nothing else carries a
   scene: no brown floors, no plain black walls, no cold steel as a feature.
2. **One light, warm.** A hidden strip grazing the stone, one key light on the
   hero object, a reflection in the polished floor. Never cold white. Strips
   glow gold; they do not clip to white.
3. **Darkness frames; it is never the subject.** In any viewport at least a
   third of the frame is lit material.
4. **One hero object per scene, large.** It fills at least 40% of the viewport
   height. No miniatures.
5. **Text on glass or on calm stone.** Never over pipes or objects. Each scene
   has a text zone and the camera composes the 3D to leave it quiet. No
   full-width black veils; text that needs a surface gets a smoked-glass panel
   with a brass hairline.
6. **Type with presence.** Nothing below 12px, body from 17px, display up to
   6rem, fewer words. Cormorant Garamond for display, Manrope for text. One
   gold italic word per headline. No kicker label above a heading: the heading
   carries itself. Numbers appear only where the order is real (the floors of
   the building, the steps of a request).
7. **The logo is sacred.** Only the official file, always on ivory stone. Gold
   in the interface is light (lines, hairlines, the accent word, the primary
   button), never ornament.

## Tokens

Defined in `app/globals.css`.

| Role | Token | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Ground | `--bg` | `#050505` |
| Text | `--text` / `--text-soft` / `--text-muted` | `#f2ebdf` / `#cdc5b7` / `#a19a8d` |
| Brass light | `--gold` / `--gold-light` / `--gold-text` | `#c9a465` / `#e2c48e` / `#dcbc7e` |
| Ivory stone | `--ivory` / `--ivory-ink` | `#ede3da` / `#151514` |
| Smoked glass | `--glass` / `--glass-edge` | panel fill and its brass hairline |
| Type scale | `--step--1` … `--step-6` | 14px label floor … 6rem display cap |

World materials live in `world/materials.ts`; the marble is generated by
`scripts/make-marble.py` and mapped in world space, so a metre of wall carries
the same veining on every page.

## Pages

A scene is one full viewport.

### Home — the Name
Job: in five seconds, who this is, what they do, where, and how to call.
1. **The Name.** The reference frame for the whole site.
2. **The Services.** The building in section stands in the right of the frame
   and the camera goes down it floor by floor; the six services stay on glass
   at the left and the row for the floor in view opens.
3. **The Standard.** "Precision in every detail." At the foot of the line the
   camera closes on the main shut-off valve. The four "why" points, short.
4. **The Work.** Three plates on ivory stone mats.
5. **The Company.** Owner-led and Greater Boston in one scene, closing on the
   call to action.

### Services — the Section
Job: show the six services and lead into each.
- The floor-by-floor descent stays. The camera goes inside each floor: the room
  fills the frame, marble behind, a warm cove, polished floor, the fixture at
  hero size.
- Seven scenes: the opening and six rooms.
- The brass line is the progress indicator, marked 01–06.
- Service detail: the same room, close on the fixture, content in a glass
  reading column.

### Our Work — the Plates
Job: photography is the subject.
- Plates at 55–65% of the viewport width, ivory stone mat, brass picture
  light, caption on a small plaque.
- Each plate is labelled "Scope of work · Illustrative" until real project
  photography exists. No apology in the headline.
- Ready for real photos: a plate becomes a project and opens `/work/[slug]`.

### About — the Plumb
Job: trust, and who answers for the work.
- Three instruments, one per word of the headline: Plumb (the bob), Level (a
  brass spirit level), True (a try square). One scene and one value each.
- Leadership: the owner's name on an ivory stone plate. No invented portrait.
- Service area closes the page.

### Contact — the Valve
Job: call, text, email or request service.
- The slot of light stays.
- The valve sits in frame beside the four actions and opens on focus.
- Without a configured form, no "coming soon": only "What happens next" in
  three steps. With one, the form sits in a glass panel.

### Shared
- Closing call to action and footer sit on the marble floor under a strip of
  light, not on a void.
- Service Areas, Privacy and 404 share one reading template: a glass column
  over the nearest place.
- Phones follow the same rules with a pre-rendered still of each scene, the
  line, and full-width panels.

## How the quality is reached

1. **Light as on the Name.** Every room has a cove strip where the back wall
   meets the ceiling, an additive wash down the marble and a pool on the
   floor (`world/wash.ts`), plus one warm point light in front of its fixture.
   Contact shadows are baked in Blender (`scripts/bake-world.py`). Full
   lightmaps were planned and dropped: near-black polished stone shows light
   as glow and reflection, which a diffuse lightmap cannot carry.
2. **World-scale marble.** One continuous texture, no mirroring.
3. **Close cameras, modelled objects.** Stations inside the rooms; fixtures
   modelled in `scripts/fixtures.py`.

The mirror floor is used on Home and Contact only.

## Out of scope

The city skyline over water, shattered stone and glass house from the owner's
reference image. The reference sets the level, not the content.

## Acceptance, per scene

- Rules 1–7 hold in captures at 1440 × 900 and 390 × 844.
  `node scripts/shots.mjs` takes them with the installed Chrome and the real GPU,
  and prints the frame rate.
- Contrast AA; keyboard focus visible; reduced motion and `?world=off` show
  all content.
- Typecheck, lint, tests and build pass.
